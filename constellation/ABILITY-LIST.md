- **Barge**: Rush into an enemy, delivering a damaging impact that forces them backward.
- **Rampage**: Heighten your rage, dealing additional damage with each attack.
- **Sunder**: Strike equipment or armor to damage it and compromise its protection.

**Knight** (Fighter line)

- **Pierce**: Deliver a focused weapon attack against a target's defenses.
- **Wall**: Defend a retreating ally against advancing enemies.
- **Errantry**: Maintain determined progress toward an assigned objective despite obstacles and interference.

**Gallant** (Fighter line)

- **Coup de Main**: A swift assault against an objective considered secure. Unlike Storm, its effectiveness comes from timing and surprise rather than brute-force entry.
- **Rampart**: Coordinate available allies and obstacles into an improvised defensive position against an advancing force.
- **Undaunted**: Maintain physical coordination and judgment under exhaustion, pain, and overwhelming pressure.

**Poet**

- **Vitriol**: Deliver a devastating verbal assault that inflicts direct psychological damage, undermining a target's composure.
- **Barb**: Deliver a concise verbal criticism that exposes a contradiction or weakens someone's credibility.
- **Impersonate**: Reproduce another person's voice to deceive listeners or issue misleading commands.
- **Goad**: Provoke an opponent into acting rashly, creating a tactical opening.
- **Recite**: Hold an audience's attention through a commanding delivery, creating opportunities for companions.

**Herald** (Poet line)

- **Cue**: Deliver a damaging weapon strike while signaling a companion to exploit the attack, giving them an attack regardless of initiative position.
- **Alarum**: Issue an immediate warning that allows companions to react to an approaching threat or attempted ambush.
- **Parley**: Establish a formal opportunity to negotiate with an adversary during an active confrontation.

**Provocateur** (Poet line)

- **Cheap Shot**: Deliver a damaging physical attack while an opponent is distracted by an argument or confrontation.
- **Implicate**: Redirect an opponent's suspicion or hostility toward another plausible culprit.
- **Discord**: Introduce credible misinformation or exploit existing mistrust to weaken an opposing group's cohesion.

**Tribune** (Poet line)

- **Quash**: Deliver a damaging physical attack against an opponent. If they are attempting to issue orders, signals, or reinforcements, you disrupt their command.
- **Veto**: Challenge an order or decision issued by an authority you have standing to oppose, potentially preventing its execution.
- **Mandate**: Turn willing individuals into an organized force capable of changing an encounter through coordinated action, without mind control or magical influence.

**Null**

- **Sense**: Detect the presence of active Magicka or Ingenium through disturbances in their operation.
- **Short**: Cause a targeted magical or technological surge to discharge its active energy against its wielder.
- **Refuse**: Reject an externally imposed magical or technical compulsion, preventing it from damaging you.

**Nil** (Null line)

- **Diminish**: Deliver a damaging physical strike that temporarily reduces the effectiveness of an active magical or technological enhancement on the target.
- **Withstand**: Maintain physical control against an incoming supernatural or technological force, reducing its ability to displace you.
- **Depletion**: Increase the energy expenditure required to sustain a targeted active working, hastening its exhaustion.

**Naught** (Null line)

- **Hollow**: Strike an opponent while weakening the active energy sustaining one of its magical or technological protections.
- **Deadzone**: Establish a limited area where active magical and technological workings become less effective.
- **Blank**: Conceal your presence from detection systems that rely upon active magical or technological signatures.

**Nix** (Null line)

- **Rebuke**: Deliver a damaging counterstrike against an opponent attempting to impose a magical or technological effect upon you.
- **Abjure**: Reject an active supernatural or technological influence affecting yourself, weakening or ending its continued effect.
- **Deny**: Interrupt an opponent's attempt to establish a new magical or technological working within your effective reach.

**Sentinaut** (Null line)

- **Nullstrike**: Deliver a concentrated damaging attack that temporarily suppresses a target's active magical or technological enhancements.
- **Aegisnull**: Establish a protective field around nearby allies that diminishes incoming magical and technologically generated damage.
- **Severlink**: Interrupt the connection between a creature and a remotely sustained magical or technological working, preventing its continued operation.

### Praethacy

**Warden**

- **Strikefield**: Release a concentrated Praethic discharge that damages a target within reach of your active field.
- **Barrier**: Establish a protective Praethic field around yourself or another creature, reducing incoming damage.
- **Groundlink**: Connect yourself to a compatible physical or energetic anchor, resisting forced displacement and disruptive effects.
- **Polarize**: Alter the alignment of an active Praethic field to attract or repel compatible objects and energetic manifestations.
- **Contain**: Enclose an unstable magical or technological working within a temporary Praethic boundary, preventing its effects from spreading.

**Psynth** (Warden line)

- **Impulse**: Deliver a damaging pulse of Praethic force that drives an opponent backward.
- **Splinter**: Project a Praethic strike that separates into multiple damaging fragments upon impact.
- **Phase**: Shift the energetic state of a targeted working, allowing an attack to bypass a physical or magical defense.
- **Bridge**: Create a temporary interface through which a magical working can operate a compatible technological mechanism, or vice versa.
- **Splice**: Connect two otherwise separate energetic pathways, allowing their effects to operate through a shared channel.

**Ghrimm** (Warden line)

- **Gridlock**: Establish an interconnected Praethic field that damages opponents attempting to move through its active boundaries.
- **Reintegrate**: Reconnect damaged components of a compatible mechanical or Praethic construct, restoring its operation.
- **Ironveil**: Reinforce a defensive Praethic field through a connected technological apparatus, reducing damage sustained by creatures within it.

**Wreicht** (Warden line)

- **Spellreave**: Deliver a damaging Praethic attack that draws upon an active magical working, weakening its manifestation.
- **Wardweave**: Extend an active magical defense through Praethic force, allowing it to protect additional nearby creatures.
- **Transposition**: Exchange the positions of two compatible magical manifestations through an established Praethic connection.
